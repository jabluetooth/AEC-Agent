### Phase 1: Signal Restoration (Solving the "Disjoint" Image)

**Goal:** Fix the specific issue visible in your "Output" image where continuous lines are broken into dashed segments.
**Analysis of your Image:** The "Output" (left) fails because standard vectorizers trace *exactly* what they see: gaps in the raster pixels. They do not "understand" that the dashed arc is meant to be a solid circle.
**Implementation Steps:**

1. **Morphological Closing:** Implement an OpenCV pre-processing step using `cv2.morphologyEx` with a `MORPH_CLOSE` kernel.
* *Why:* This mathematically "dilates" (fattens) the white pixels to bridge the gaps seen in your left image, then "erodes" them back to the original size, leaving a continuous line `[1]`.


2. **Gaussian Blur & Threshold:** Apply a slight Gaussian Blur followed by Adaptive Thresholding (`cv2.adaptiveThreshold`).
* *Why:* This smooths out the "staircase" jagged edges visible in your output, preparing it for smoother vectorization.



### Phase 2: The "Split-Stream" Engine (Core Logic)

https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics

**Goal:** Separate "Geometry" (Walls/Wires) from "Semantics" (Valves/Alarms) to prevent the "bag of lines" mess.
**Implementation Steps:**

1. **Stream A: Symbol Detection (YOLOv8)**
* **Action:** Train a YOLOv8 model on MEP symbols (Fire Alarms, Data Ports).
* **Logic:** Run inference on the PDF. If an object is found (e.g., "Smoke Detector"), record its `(x, y)` coordinates and **erase** it from the image (fill with black).
* *Result:* This removes the complex symbols that confuse vectorizers, leaving only clean lines for Stream B.


2. **Stream B: Geometric Vectorization**
* **Action:** Process the "erased" image using the **Deep Vectorization of Technical Drawings** model `[2]`.
* **Primitive Fitting:** Instead of tracing contours, this model predicts *primitives*. It will look at the pixel cluster of your "broken circle" and output a mathematical `CIRCLE(center, radius)` entity, ensuring the output matches the "Reference" image on the right.

### Phase 3: Topology Reconstruction (The "Wiring" Phase)

**Goal:** Ensure the lines connect electrically (for MEP/LV circuits) rather than just visually.
**Implementation Steps:**

1. **Skeletonization:** Use `scikit-image.morphology.skeletonize` to reduce the thick wire lines to 1-pixel-wide centerlines `[3]`.
2. **Graph Construction:** Ingest these lines into **NetworkX**. Treat line endpoints as "Nodes" and lines as "Edges".
3. **Degree-2 Merging:** Iterate through the graph. If a node has exactly 2 edges (a "knee" or a fake break in a straight line), remove the node and merge the edges. This programmatically heals the fragmentation seen in your left image `[4]`.
4. **Endpoint Snapping:** If a line ends within 5 pixels of another line (a "dangling node"), snap it to the nearest neighbor to close the circuit `[5]`.

https://github.com/abey79/vpype
