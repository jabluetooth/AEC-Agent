### 2. Implementation Plan: The "Hybrid" MEP Pipeline

To achieve professional automation, you cannot use a single model. You must use a "Split-Stream" pipeline: one stream for symbols (Deep Learning) and one for wiring/pipes (Vectorization).

#### **Step 1: Input & Signal Conditioning**

* **Goal:** Convert PDF to high-quality raster images free of noise.
* **Tool:** `pdf2image` + `OpenCV`.
* **Action:**
* Convert PDF to PNG at **300 DPI** (minimum).
* Apply **Morphological Closing** (`cv2.morphologyEx` with `MORPH_CLOSE`) to bridge small gaps in the black ink *before* processing. This physically connects the broken pixels in your reference image.



#### **Step 2: The "Symbol" Stream (Object Detection)**

* **Goal:** Identify Alarm/MEP symbols (circles, squares, triangles) so they don't get vectorized as jagged lines.
* **Tool:** **YOLOv8** (Fine-tuned).
* **Dataset:** Use the **([https://universe.roboflow.com/yolo-nas-frb7l/mep-elements-in-construction-site](https://universe.roboflow.com/yolo-nas-frb7l/mep-elements-in-construction-site))** (Roboflow) or generate synthetic data by placing standard CAD blocks onto random backgrounds.
* **Implementation:**
1. Run inference on the raster plan.
2. Get Bounding Boxes: `(Class: "Smoke_Detector", x: 100, y: 200)`.
3. **Crucial Step:** **Inpaint/Erase** these regions from the raster image. Fill them with white. This prevents the line vectorizer (Step 3) from trying to trace the complex symbol, which causes the "messy" output.



#### **Step 3: The "Geometry" Stream (Vectorization)**

* **Goal:** Convert the remaining wires, pipes, and walls into vectors.
* **Tool:** **Deep Vectorization of Technical Drawings**.
* **Implementation:**
* Use their **Pre-trained Primitives Model**. It inputs the "erased" raster from Step 2.
* **Output:** It returns a list of primitives: `(type: circle, center: x,y, radius: r)` and `(type: line, x1, y1, x2, y2)`.
* *Note:* Unlike Potrace, this model "hallucinates" the perfect shape, fixing the "disjoint" issue in your screenshot.



#### **Step 4: Topology Regularization (The "clean up")**

* **Goal:** Connect lines that *almost* touch and force them to be straight.
* **Tool:** **`vpype`** (CLI or Python API) or **`shreg`**.
* **Implementation:**
```python
import vpype
# Load your raw vector data
doc = vpype.read_svg("raw_output.svg")
# 1. Merge lines with gaps < 5 pixels
vpype.execute(doc, "linemerge --tolerance 5px")
# 2. Simplify jagged lines
vpype.execute(doc, "linesimplify")

```



#### **Step 5: DXF Assembly**

* **Goal:** Combine the detected Symbols and the Vectorized Lines into one AutoCAD file.
* **Tool:** **`ezdxf`**.
* **Implementation:**
* Create a new DXF document.
* **Insert Symbols:** Iterate through YOLO results (Step 2). For every "Smoke Detector," insert a corresponding `.dwg` Block at those coordinates.
* **Draw Lines:** Iterate through vectorized lines (Step 4). Draw them on appropriate layers (e.g., `E-ALARM-WIRING`).
